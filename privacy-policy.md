Privacy Policy for Ahlan Social
Last Updated: October 13, 2026
Welcome to Ahlan Social ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.
1. Information We Collect
To provide you with a smooth and secure social experience, we collect only the necessary data:
Account Information: Username and email address used for registration and login.
Profile Details: Optional profile pictures, bios, or social links you choose to add.
User Content: Photos, videos, and comments you share on the platform.
Location Data: Approximate or precise location information (if permitted by your device settings) to provide location-based features and relevant contextual content or advertisements.
Technical Data: Basic device information and app version used strictly for troubleshooting, performance monitoring, and technical support.
2. Device Permissions
Ahlan Social requests only the minimum permissions required for core app functionality:
Camera: Used strictly to allow you to capture photos and record videos within the app.
Location: Used to deliver location-relevant content, features, and targeted advertisements.
Notifications & Vibration: Used to send you timely updates regarding likes, comments, and interactions.
Note: We do not access your external storage, media files via broad permissions, microphone, or display over other apps.
3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information exclusively to:
Provide, maintain, and improve the Ahlan Social app and its features.
Authenticate your account and ensure platform security.
Display relevant, personalized, or location-based advertisements and sponsored content.
Send essential service updates and push notifications.
Comply with legal obligations.
4. Third-Party Services & Advertising
We work with trusted third-party service providers (such as cloud storage, authentication, push notification, and advertising partners) to operate our app and display ads. These partners may use cookies, device identifiers, or location data to serve relevant advertisements. These third parties are bound by strict confidentiality agreements and privacy standards.
5. Data Security & Retention
Security: We use industry-standard encryption (such as HTTPS/TLS) to protect your data in transit and at rest. However, no electronic transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
Retention: Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal data and content are permanently removed from our systems.
6. Your Rights & Account Deletion
You retain full control over your personal data. Through the app settings, you can:
Access or update your profile information.
Manage your location and advertising preferences.
Request the complete deletion of your account and associated data at any time.
7. Children's Privacy
Ahlan Social is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app, and significant updates will be communicated to you clearly.
